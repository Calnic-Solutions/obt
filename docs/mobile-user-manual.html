User Handbook · Phone app

Welcome to the LIVUKA phone app

The same guided workspace as the website, on a phone that works without the internet.

This manual shows how to do your work in the LIVUKA Android app: where to tap, what each screen is for, and how to move a Set to the next stage. Every stage follows the same rules as the website, so a Set can be worked on the phone and on the website by turns.

The big difference from the website is the internet is only needed twice: when you sign in and download your work, and when you send your finished work back with the Send bar on the Home screen. Everything in between - listening, recording, questions, answers, reviews - happens on the phone, wherever you are.

The demos in this manual are small animated copies of the app’s screens: watch the hand tap, then do the same on your phone. The blue or orange glow around a button means the app is showing you the next step: that button pulses on the phone until you tap it.

Follow the Journey of a Set

A Set is one part of a book. From the first recording to the final approval, it moves through eight stages. At every stop, the right person completes the work before the Set moves forward.

1. DraftDrafter - record every passage and choose the best take.
2. Peer CheckPeer Checker - listen to the whole recording. Is it correct?
3. Community SetupDrafter - prepare the questions for the community.
4. Community TestDrafter - ask the questions and record the answers.
5. Community ReviewPeer Checker - read the answers. Did the people understand?
6. Back TranslationBack Translator - say the recording back in the source language.
7. Consultant CheckConsultant - compare the two. Approve, or send it back.
8. ApprovedThe work is done. The Set is listed under Approved.
Work can come back. The Peer Checker, the Community Review and the Consultant can send a Set back with a comment. The Drafter records the passage again and sends it forward. The phone shows exactly what was said and where.
1

Start Here: Sign In and Download

The First Step - internet needed
  1. Connect the phone to the internetWi-Fi or mobile data. Signing in downloads all the audio and scripts for your Sets, so a good connection saves time.
  2. Open the LIVUKA appTap the LIVUKA icon on the phone: the orange smile on navy. Wait for the Welcome back screen.
  3. Enter your email and passwordTap each box and type. The eye icon shows the password while you check it.
  4. Tap Sign inThe app now collects your data: every Set, script and recording assigned to you. A bar shows the files downloaded so far. Keep the app open until it reaches 100%.

If some files did not download

A slow connection can stop a few files. The app then lists every file it could not get: which Set and passage it belongs to, what it is used for, and what went wrong. For each one you choose:

  1. Try againDownloads that file once more. Try all again and the big button at the bottom do it for every file at once.
  2. SkipGoes on without the file. A skipped file is fetched at the next Check for updates, so nothing is lost - but a passage whose source audio is missing cannot be recorded until then.
You sign in every time you open the app. With internet the server checks your password; without it the phone checks it against your last sign-in, and your work is there as you left it. The first sign-in on a phone needs internet - that is when your work is downloaded (see Settings and Sign out).
Need help signing in? If you cannot sign in, contact your project administrator.

The Home Screen

Workbench - one tile for each kind of work

After the download you land on the Workbench. Each tile is one list of Sets waiting for you. The tiles you see depend on your roles:

Sent backDrafter. Sets returned to you with comments. The red badge shows how long ago the last one came back.
DraftDrafter. Sets to record - new ones and ones you have started.
Community Setup / Community TestDrafter. Sets waiting for questions, or for the community’s answers.
ApprovedDrafter. Sets the Consultant has approved. Nothing more to do.
Peer Check / Community ReviewPeer Checker. Sets waiting for your check.
Back TranslationBack Translator. Sets waiting for a back translation.
Consultant CheckConsultant. Sets waiting for approval.
  1. Tap a tileOpens the list of Sets in it. The tile with the pulsing glow is where the app suggests you go next.
  2. Tap a SetOpens the Set. What you see there depends on the stage - the steps below show each one.
  3. The bar at the bottomSend (3) means three Sets have changes waiting on the phone. Slide the orange knob to the right to send them. Check for updates means nothing is waiting; sliding it fetches new work from the server. The bar is hidden while the phone has no internet. See Send your work and Check for updates.
  4. The three dotsOpen Settings: your roles, app language, storage, sync status and Sign out.
Nothing assigned yet? The Workbench says Nothing assigned to you yet. Ask your admin. Ask your administrator to assign you a Set, then slide Check for updates.

Ready to begin? Tap the tile that glows.

Your Work as a Drafter

As a Drafter, you bring the translated Word of God to life through clear, careful audio recordings. On the phone your journey begins with Draft and continues through the community stages until the Set is approved.

DraftRecord every passage and choose the best take.
Community SetupPrepare the questions that will be used with the community.
Community TestAsk the questions and record the community’s answers.
ApprovedSee the Sets that have finished the whole journey.

Other members of the team support your work along the way: Peer Checker → Community Review → Back Translator → Consultant Check.

Work can come back. A Set may be returned by the Peer Checker, by Community Review or by the Back Translator. This is a normal part of the work. You record the passage again and send it forward. See Step 4.
3

Draft: Record the Passages

Home → Draft → a Set

Open a Set

  1. Tap Draft on the Home screenThe list shows every Set you can record. Jump to a pending work item at the top takes you straight to the next Set with work left.
  2. Tap the SetThe Set page lists its passages. Each passage shows its state: Not started, 2 takes, Finalized.
  3. Tap Record on a passageOpens the passage. Start with the one that glows.

Listen and read first

The passage page has the source audio, the source script and the target script at the top, and the take strip and Record button at the bottom. The microphone stays grey until you have done three things:

  1. Play the source audio to the endTap the play triangle. You may pause, but the recording has to reach the end once. The text next to the microphone tells you what is still missing.
  2. Tick “I have read” under the source scriptThe box glows when it is your next step.
  3. Tick “I have read” under the target scriptScroll down inside the card if you do not see it. The microphone turns orange.
Only once per round. The phone remembers that you heard the source and read the scripts. Coming back tomorrow to record take three, you go straight to the microphone.

Record a take

  1. Tap the orange microphoneThe first time, Android asks Allow LIVUKA to record audio? Tap While using the app. Recording starts at once: a red clock counts the seconds and a dotted line moves with your voice.
  2. Speak the passageHold the phone close, in a quiet room. Use the orange pause button if you need a breath; tap it again to go on.
  3. Tap the red stop buttonThe take is saved and the Automated QC runs on it at once.

Read the Automated QC

Every take is checked for clipping, silence at the start and end, noise, length against the source, whether a voice was found, even volume and background noise. Green ticks pass; red crosses tell you what to fix. The result is a pill on the take: QC: Good or QC: Bad.

A bad take cannot be the final take. You may keep it to compare, but the star stays grey. Record again until a take passes.

Choose the final take

Up to six takes fit in the strip under the scripts. A green tick marks a saved take. Tap any square to open the Takes page.

  1. Listen to each takeEvery take has its own player. The magnifier button re-runs the QC and shows its details.
  2. Tap the star on the best takeIt turns green and the take is marked Final. Tap the star again to un-mark it. Not synced means the take is still only on this phone.
  3. Delete takes you do not needThe red bin asks Remove this take? This cannot be undone. A deleted take is gone from the phone and, after the next Send, from the server.
  4. Go to the next passageUse the round › arrow at the edge of the screen, or the back arrow to the Set page. The arrow glows once the passage has its final take.

Send the Set to Peer Check

  1. Finish every passageBack on the Set page, a finished passage says Finalized and offers Re-record. The grey note counts what is left; the bottom button says 3 passages left until then.
  2. Play the Target Recording to the endWhen all passages are final, the Set page shows the Target Recording - all final takes joined together. Play it through once. Tap the waveform to jump to a spot.
  3. Tap Send to Peer CheckConfirm with Send. The Set leaves your Draft list. It reaches the Peer Checker after your next Send from the Home screen.
Then send from Home. Moving a Set on the phone is only half the job. Go back to the Home screen and slide Send when you have internet - only then does the Peer Checker see it. See Send your work to the server.

Every passage final, the recording heard, the Set sent: the Draft is done.

4

Sent back: Fix What Was Marked

Home → Sent back → a Set

When the Peer Checker, the Community Review or the Back Translator finds a problem, the Set comes back to you with comments. The Sent back tile on Home shows a red badge with how long ago it arrived.

  1. Tap Sent back, then the SetThe red pill on the card says which stage returned it.
  2. Read the feedback at the top of the Set pageA red box lists every comment. From Community Review it shows the question, the community’s answer and the reviewer’s reply, with a play button for each voice message. Passages that need a new take have a red outline.
  3. Open the passage and find the pinTap Re-record. On the Takes page the old final take is marked Sent back with a red note explaining why. A red pin on its waveform marks the exact spot of the comment - tap the pin to read or hear it.
  4. Record a new take and make it finalThe same as in Draft: record, pass the QC, tap the star. The old take stays for comparison until you delete it.
  5. Send the Set on againPlay the Target Recording to the end and tap the send button. A Set returned by the Back Translator goes straight back to the Back Translator; the others go to Peer Check. Then slide Send on Home.
Only the marked passages need a new take - unless your project sends back the whole Set, in which case every passage says Part of a whole-Set send back and needs one.
5

Community Setup: Prepare the Questions

Home → Community Setup → a Set

The Peer Checker has passed the Set. Now you prepare the questions you will ask the community, so you can find out whether the recording is understood.

  1. Play the Target RecordingListen for the places where a question belongs. Tap the waveform to move to a spot.
  2. Tap Add a question hereThe question is pinned at the current position of the player (the sheet says Pinned at 0:08). Choose Voice and tap the red microphone to record it, or Text and type it. Tap Save.
  3. Tap Add a general question for a question about the whole SetIt is not pinned to a spot. General questions are listed under the player; the red bin removes one you added by mistake.
  4. Tap Send to Community TestConfirm. Then slide Send on the Home screen.
A new round starts empty. When a Set comes back to Community Setup after a send-back, the earlier round’s questions are closed and no longer listed - the same rule as the website. Ask new questions for the new recording.
6

Community Test: Ask and Record the Answers

Home → Community Test → a Set - with the community

Sit with the community. Play them the recording, ask each question, and record what they say. No internet is needed for any of this.

  1. Play the Target Recording to the endThe questions stay hidden until the community has heard the whole recording once. Question pins show on the waveform where each question belongs.
  2. Tap Answer under a questionThe sheet shows the question. Record the community’s answer with Voice, or type it with Text, then Save. The card turns green: Answered.
  3. Answer every questionThe bottom button counts what is left (1 question left). It becomes Send to Community Review when all are answered.
  4. Tap Send to Community ReviewConfirm, then slide Send on Home when you have internet again.
7

Approved: The Journey Is Complete

Home → Approved

When the Consultant approves a Set it moves to the Approved tile. Open it to see the Set’s passages and listen to the approved recording. There is nothing more to record.

Downloading the audio is done on the website, from the Finished list, where the files can be saved to a computer.

The Peer Checker’s Work

As a Peer Checker you are the second pair of ears. You hear the Drafter’s recording against the source and decide whether it may go on to the community, and later whether the community understood it.

Peer CheckListen to the source and the recording. Pass it on, or send it back with a note.
Community ReviewRead the community’s answers. Like each one, or dislike it and say why.
8

Peer Check: Listen and Decide

Home → Peer Check → a Set

The page shows the Source script, the Target script, the Source audio and the Target Recording. The scripts follow the passage that is playing.

  1. Play the Source audio to the endThe play button glows until you have.
  2. Play the Target Recording to the endListen for anything wrong. Both recordings must be heard through before the Set can go forward.
  3. Found a problem? Tap Add a note hereThe note is pinned at the current position of the target recording (At 0:04). Say it with Voice or type it with Text, then Save. A pin appears on the waveform; tap it to review or delete the note.
  4. Tap Send back to the DrafterOnly possible when at least one note is saved. The Drafter sees each note on the take it was made on.
  5. Or tap Send to Community SetupWhen both recordings have been heard and there are no notes. Confirm, then slide Send on Home.
9

Community Review: Did They Understand?

Home → Community Review → a Set

Each card shows a question from the Community Test and the answer the community gave. Voice questions and answers have a play button.

  1. Play the Target RecordingThe question pins show where each question was asked.
  2. Read or hear every answerAsk yourself: does this answer show the people understood the passage?
  3. Tap Like or Dislike on each cardDislike opens What is wrong with this answer? Record or type what the Drafter should fix, then Save. You may change your comment afterwards.
  4. Tap Send back to the DrafterAvailable once at least one answer is disliked. The Drafter sees the question, the answer and your reply together.
  5. Or tap Send to Back TranslationAvailable once every answer is liked. Confirm, then slide Send on Home.
A question with no answer blocks both buttons - the Set has to go back to the Community Test for that answer first. The grey line above the buttons tells you.

The Back Translator’s work

As a Back Translator you listen to the target recording and say or write what it means in the source language, without looking at the source. The Consultant uses your back translation to judge the recording.

Back translationHear the recording, write or record the back translation, submit it to the Consultant.
Only the target audio is downloaded for this role - no source audio, no questions - so signing in is quick.
10

Back translation: Say It Back

Home → Back Translation → a Set

The page shows the Target Recording at the top and a Back translation card under it (text or voice - either is fine).

  1. Play the Target Recording to the endThe submit button stays off until the whole recording has been heard.
  2. Write or record the back translationType into Type the back translation..., or tap Tap to record audio and speak it. You may do both. The text and the recording are saved on the phone as you go.
  3. Not satisfied with the recording? Comment on itTap Not satisfied? Comment on it under the player. The comment is pinned at the current spot. With an open comment you can Send back to the Drafter; the Drafter fixes the passage and the Set comes straight back to you.
  4. Tap Submit to ConsultantConfirm with Submit. Then slide Send on Home.

When the Consultant sends it back

The card The Consultant sent this back lists the Consultant’s comments. Tap Reply under a comment to answer it (voice or text). Then either submit a new back translation - which closes all of those comments - or send the Set back to the Drafter if the recording itself is the problem.

Open comments block Submit. If you left a comment on the recording, send the Set back to the Drafter or delete the comment before submitting.

The Consultant’s Work

As a Consultant you have the last word. You compare the back translation with the source, and either approve the Set or send it back to the Back Translator with your comments.

Consultant checkCompare, comment, approve or send back.
11

Consultant check: Approve or Send Back

Home → Consultant Check → a Set

The page shows, from the top: the Source script, the Source audio, the Target script, the Target Recording, and the Back translation with its text and recording. Under it are Your comments and the Back Translator’s replies.

  1. Play the back translation recording to the endIf the Back Translator recorded one, Approve waits until it has been heard.
  2. Compare it with the sourcePlay the Source audio and read the scripts as you need.
  3. Something wrong? Tap Comment on the back translationVoice or text, then Save. Add as many as you need; the bin deletes your own.
  4. Tap Send back to the Back TranslatorWith at least one comment. Confirm with Send back. Your comments stay with the Set; when it returns you see the earlier round in grey and the Back Translator’s replies under each comment.
  5. Or tap ApproveOnly when there is no open comment. Confirm with Approve: the Set is finished. Then slide Send on Home.

Help

How the manual is arranged, and how the parts of the app that appear on every screen work.

How to use the manual

  1. Find your roleThe menu on the left (the Steps button on a small screen) lists the stages under each role. Open the one you are working on.
  2. Follow the numbered stepsEach step names the button to tap and what happens next. The demos beside them show the same taps on a phone.
  3. Look for the glowOn the phone, the next thing to tap pulses with a soft glow. In the demos you can see it as a halo around a button.
  4. SearchType a word in the search box at the top to jump to every place it appears.

The top bar

Every screen inside a Set has the same bar at the top:

Back arrowOne screen back: passage → Set → list → Home. The phone’s own back gesture does the same.
TitleThe Set or passage name, with the stage or the verse range under it.
EN | SRCSwitches the Set names between English and the source language.
HomeStraight back to the Workbench from anywhere.
Three dotsOpens Settings (see below).

The audio player

Every recording - source audio, take, target recording, voice comment - uses the same player.

Play / pauseThe triangle starts; it becomes a pause sign while playing. Starting one player pauses any other.
WaveformTap anywhere on the bars to jump to that spot. The played part is coloured. The clock shows the position and the length.
1x ▾ SpeedTap for 1x, 2x, 3x or 5x. Useful when checking a long recording. A faster copy is prepared the first time; the change may take a moment.
Full screenOpens the Audio Review Player: a bigger waveform to find a spot exactly, with the same controls.
PinsA round marker above the waveform is a comment or question pinned at that spot. Tap it to read or hear it.
“Play to the end” rules count only when the recording reaches its end. Jumping ahead with a tap on the waveform does not count; pausing and continuing does. Playing at 2x or 5x counts.

Script view

The Source script and Target script cards show the text for the passage that is open or playing. On stage pages with several passages, the scripts follow the recording as it plays.

ExpandOpens the script full screen with bigger text, for reading while you record.
I have readOn the Draft passage page only. Both boxes must be ticked before the first take of a round.

Send your work to the server

Home screen, the bar at the bottom - internet needed

Everything you do is saved on the phone first: takes, final choices, comments, questions, answers, verdicts, back translations and every stage move. Nothing leaves the phone until you send it. Until then nobody else - no Peer Checker, no Consultant, no one on the website - can see it.

  1. Go to the Home screenTap the house icon in any top bar. The sending bar lives only on Home, at the bottom.
  2. Read the numberSend (3) means three Sets have changes that are only on this phone. The same number shows in Settings → Sync status.
  3. Get internetThe bar shows only while the phone is connected. Without internet you see a grey line instead: No network. 3 changes are waiting to be sent. Your work waits safely until you are connected.
  4. Slide the orange knob to the rightDrag it all the way across. The bar says Checking with the server..., then Sending..., then Checking for updates... - sending always finishes with an update check, so anything new for you comes down in the same go.
  5. Keep the app openStay on the screen until the message appears. Closing the app halfway stops the send; nothing is lost, and the next slide sends the rest.
  6. Read the messageSent. 3 Sets reached the server. Nothing new to download. The bar then reads Check for updates, because nothing is waiting any more.

When a Set was not sent

If the server refused a Set, the message says so - 1 Set was not sent - and the Sync status screen opens by itself. You can also open it any time from the three dots → Sync status.

  1. Read the cardEach Set shows what is still only on the phone - 2 recordings · 1 comment · hand-off to Peer Check - and a label: Waiting (not sent yet) or Not sent (the server said no). A red box gives the server’s own reason.
  2. Fix it and send againMost reasons mend themselves: a weak signal, a Set that moved on while you were offline. Tap Send now at the bottom, or slide Send on Home.
  3. Save recordingsCopies the Set’s unsent recordings to the phone’s Downloads folder, so they are safe even if the Set can never be sent.
  4. Give up this workShown only when the server will never accept the Set. It throws the unsent work away - save the recordings first, and ask your manager before you tap it.
Signing out keeps your work. Your Sets, recordings and anything not yet sent stay on the phone, locked, until you sign in again. Settings shows Everything has been sent. once nothing is waiting.

Check for updates

Home screen, the bar at the bottom - internet needed

The phone does not look for new work on its own. When your administrator assigns you a Set, or a checker sends one back, it reaches the phone only when you ask. The bar reads Check for updates whenever nothing is waiting to be sent.

  1. Go to the Home screen with internet onIf the bar says Send (N) instead, slide it anyway - a send always ends with an update check.
  2. Slide the knob to the rightThe bar says Checking with the server..., then Downloading files... with a count while the new source audio and scripts come down.
  3. Read the messageEverything is up to date. means nothing changed. Downloaded 3 new files. means new work arrived - look at the tiles: a new Set raises a tile’s count, and a returned Set puts a red badge on Sent back.
  4. If a file did not downloadA list opens naming each file, the Set it belongs to and why it stopped. Try again fetches it once more; Skip goes on without it, and it is tried again at the next check (see Start Here).

What a check brings

New SetsSets assigned to you since the last check, with their source audio and scripts.
Sets sent backSets a checker returned, with their comments. They appear on the Sent back tile.
Sets that moved onA Set you handed off that was accepted leaves your tile; one that another person finished moves to the stage that is now yours.
Skipped filesAny file skipped at sign-in or at an earlier check is tried again.
Sets no longer yoursA Set reassigned to someone else is removed from the phone, with its files, so storage is not wasted.
When to check. At the start of each working day, whenever your administrator says a Set is ready, and after a checker tells you something came back. A check costs only the files that are new.

Settings and Sign out

Three dots, top right of any screen
Your name, languages and rolesWhat the server knows about you. Ask your administrator to change them.
App languageEnglish, Urdu or Hindi for the app’s own words. Set names and scripts keep their language.
Storage usedHow much space the downloaded audio takes.
Sync statusChanges waiting to be sent, and the reason any Set was refused last time.
Sign outAsks Are you sure you want to sign out? and takes you to the sign-in screen. Nothing is deleted: your work stays on the phone, locked, until you sign in again. The app also signs you out by itself after 15 minutes without a touch.

Button reference

RecordOrange round microphone on the passage page: starts a take. Grey means something must be done first - read the text beside it.
Pause / StopWhile recording. Stop saves the take and runs the QC.
QCOn a take: runs the Automated QC again and shows every check.
StarMarks the final take. Green when chosen; grey with a reason when the take cannot be final.
BinDeletes a take, a note, a question or a comment you made. Always asks first.
Re-recordOn a finalized passage: opens it to record a new take.
Send to…Moves the Set to the next stage. Grey with a reason until the stage’s rules are met.
Send back to…Returns the Set with your comments. Needs at least one comment or dislike.
Like / DislikeYour verdict on a community answer.
Voice / TextIn every sheet: record with the red microphone, or type. Save when done; the X discards.
Slide barOn Home: Send or Check for updates. Slide, do not tap.

Mandatory rules

The app enforces the same rules as the website. A grey button always shows its reason in the line above it.

DraftHear the source to the end and tick both I have read boxes before the first take. Every passage needs a final take. Only a take with QC: Good can be final. Play the Target Recording to the end before sending.
Sent backEvery marked passage needs a new final take before the Set can go on.
Community TestHear the recording to the end before the questions appear. Every question needs an answer.
Peer CheckHear both recordings to the end to send on; leave a note to send back.
Community ReviewLike every answer to send on; dislike at least one (with a reason) to send back. An unanswered question blocks both.
Back translationHear the recording to the end; write or record the back translation; no open comment of your own.
Consultant checkHear the back translation recording; no open comment to approve; at least one to send back.
SendingA finished Set is sent on its own when there is internet - right after you hand it on, or at the next check. Slide Send to send everything at once and fetch new work.

Troubleshooting

The microphone button is greyRead the text beside it: play the source to the end, tick I have read, or all six takes are used - delete one.
“Microphone is off”Android was told not to allow recording. Tap Open settings, allow the microphone for LIVUKA, and come back.
QC says no voice was detectedThe take is silent or very quiet. Hold the phone closer, speak clearly, and record again.
The waveform does not appear at onceLong recordings take a moment to draw the first time. Playing works meanwhile.
The Send bar is missingThe phone has no internet. Connect and it comes back.
A Set would not sendSettings → Sync status shows the server’s reason. Usually the Set was changed on the website meanwhile; check for updates, then send again.
Some files did not downloadChoose Try again or Skip in the list. Skipped files come with the next Check for updates.
A Set disappeared from my listIt moved on to the next stage, was sent back to someone else, or is no longer assigned to you. Check for updates to be sure.
The app was closed while recordingOpen the passage again: the recording made so far is recovered and saved as a take.

Glossary

SetOne part of a book: a few passages that travel through the stages together.
PassageOne verse range inside a Set. Each passage is recorded on its own.
TakeOne recording of a passage. Up to six per passage.
Final takeThe take chosen with the star. Only final takes go forward.
Automated QCThe checks run on every take: clipping, silence, noise, length, voice, volume, background.
Target RecordingAll final takes of a Set played as one.
SourceThe audio and script the translation is made from.
PinA comment or question fixed to one spot on a recording.
Sent backA Set returned to an earlier stage with comments.
RoundOne pass through the community stages. A send-back starts a new round with fresh questions.
Send / Check for updatesThe slide bar on Home that exchanges work with the server.
Not syncedSomething only this phone has so far.

That is the whole app. Sign in, download, work anywhere, send when you are back online.
